For the fifth consecutive year, customers have recognized Delta with the Best Airline Staff Service in North America award at the Skytrax World Airline Awards. Based entirely on customer feedback, the award recognizes the service travelers experience at the airport and onboard. At the heart of that experience is Delta people, whose care and commitment shape millions of customer journeys each year.
