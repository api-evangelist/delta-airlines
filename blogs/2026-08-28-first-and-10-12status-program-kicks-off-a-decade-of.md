Fall in Seattle means the city's best season is back at Lumen Field. As the Seahawks return in 2026 to defend their Championship title, every yard the Seahawks throw for will send Delta miles to the fans cheering them on. Delta, the Official Airline of the Seattle Seahawks, is reopening enrollment in 12Status, the Washington-based SkyMiles program named for the Seahawks' fanbase, with members earning one mile for every Seahawks passing yard at home and on the road throughout the 2026–27 season.
