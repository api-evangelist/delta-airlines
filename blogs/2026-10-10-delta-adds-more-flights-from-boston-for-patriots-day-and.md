Patriots’ Day is one of the most anticipated times of year in Massachusetts, bringing together a school vacation week and the unofficial start of spring. In 2027, Delta, the number one airline in Boston, is making it easier for travelers to turn the holiday into a warm-weather getaway. The expanded schedule builds on strong demand, adding over 45 round-trip flights and 6,000 seats.
