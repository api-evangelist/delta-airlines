When Captain Mark Cokewell pushed back from the gate for his final Delta flight on Sept. 5, it marked the culmination of more than four decades in commercial aviation. While it was the A350 captain’s final flight for Delta, it was his first with daughter flight attendant Bethany Wish on the same crew.
