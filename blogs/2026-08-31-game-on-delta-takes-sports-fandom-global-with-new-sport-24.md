On long-haul international flights, customers can cross oceans and time zones while major sports moments unfold back home and around the world. Beginning this fall, Delta will give customers on international flights a new way to watch those moments—live from their own devices. Through a new partnership with global sports marketing agency IMG, Delta Sync Wi-Fi will offer access to global sports programming via Sport 24’s live sports channels on international routes to and from the U.S.
