With nearly 1,300 Wi‑Fi-equipped aircraft already flying around the world—more than 95% of its fleet—Delta is preparing for the next chapter in its connectivity journey: the airline has reached a major milestone receiving regulatory approval to extend fast, free Delta Sync Wi‑Fi across its trans-Pacific routes, bringing customers one step closer to staying connected around the globe. The rollout will begin later this month, expanding progressively across routes in the following weeks.
