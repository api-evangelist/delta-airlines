Delta is giving customers in Salt Lake City a new way to explore the American Southwest with the launch of nonstop service to Flagstaff, Arizona (FLG), beginning June 7, 2027. A new gateway to the Southwest The new route will operate twice daily, year-round, providing convenient access to Flagstaff, known for its year-round outdoor recreation, vibrant historic downtown and some of the country's best stargazing.
