Delta’s steadfast commitment to life-giving blood drives made the airline the No. 1 corporate blood drive sponsor of the American Red Cross for the ninth consecutive year, with employees, retirees and customers contributing 15,911 units of blood in fiscal year 2026 (FYE 6/30). “Just last week, the Red Cross declared an emergency blood shortage and is urgently seeking blood donors," said Anne McKeough, Chief Development Officer for the American Red Cross.
