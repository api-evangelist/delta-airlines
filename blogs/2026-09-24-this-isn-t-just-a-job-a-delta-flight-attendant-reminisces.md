Every day at work is an opportunity for flight attendant Sandra Griego to connect with people, whether it’s her sister (also a flight attendant), fellow Los Angeles Dodgers fans, lovers of Paris – or any other opportunity that comes about to forge a bond. But one of her proudest moments came during Operation Desert Storm, when she was a part of a crew summoned during a London layover to usher a group of U.S. Marines on their way home from Kuwait.
