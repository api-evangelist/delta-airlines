A Clayton County nonprofit that delivers food directly to Georgia families in need is expanding its ability to serve the community, with support from Delta. Since its founding in 2018 by CEO Frederick West, The Online Food Pantry Mobile Food Market has helped more than 80,000 people access food assistance by recovering surplus food from local partners and delivering it to residents across six metro Atlanta counties.
