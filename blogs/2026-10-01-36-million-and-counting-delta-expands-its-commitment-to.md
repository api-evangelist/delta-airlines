For more than two decades, Delta and the Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF) have flown side by side, raising $36 million to advance research and support people impacted by breast cancer. This year, Delta is doubling down on that commitment, providing even more opportunities for customers and employees to engage with the cause. “For so many of us, breast cancer is personal,” said Kristen Manion Taylor, Delta's Senior Vice President of Brand Experience.
