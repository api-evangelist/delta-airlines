Europe is calling, and Delta is adding more ways and more time to experience it. New nonstop service from Detroit to Athens and Boston to Venice will join Delta’s previously announced route from Austin to Paris. The airline is also extending its transatlantic season, with several popular routes starting earlier in the spring or continuing deeper into the fall and winter.
