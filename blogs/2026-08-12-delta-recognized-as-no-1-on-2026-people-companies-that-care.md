Delta's people-first commitment has earned the No. 1 spot on the PEOPLE Companies that Care list , which recognizes the organizations that go above and beyond to support employees, communities and the world around them. The highly competitive PEOPLE Companies that Care list is based on over 1.3 million employee survey responses and data from companies representing the experience of more than 8.4 million employees.
